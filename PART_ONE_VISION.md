# 600-word product vision

## 1. What Kivi becomes

Kivi's main function is to act as a personal operating layer across a user's work — a copilot that understands what is going on in their conversations, documents, email, meetings and calendar. Dictation is one useful tool within that system, not the system itself. Gradually, "Hey Kivi" becomes the primary way people interact with their work: a single place to ask questions, make decisions and take action. People tend to compare Kivi with Jarvis, the key difference being that Kivi stays traceable, consent-based and under the user's control.

## 2. The problem

Work today is made up of separate parts. A decision is made in Slack, a promise is sent by email, a deadline is added to Calendar, background is discussed in a meeting, and the plan is written in Docs. None of it is connected, so people restate the same context in every tool, copy and paste between applications, and forget who is responsible for what and when it is due. Two conflicting deadlines can sit side by side unnoticed. Current assistants answer confidently without ever showing where the answer came from.

## 3. The role of semantic memory

Semantic memory turns reaction into continuity. It lets Kivi hold something learned in one situation and use it appropriately in another: bringing up a previous decision, raising a conflict rather than quietly choosing a side, or preparing a project update based on actual recorded obstacles. The same holds for personal preferences. If Kivi has repeatedly observed that someone prefers black clothing, it can show black options first when they shop. A single black item in a search history is not a preference, only a coincidence.

## 4. What Kivi should remember

Durable facts: names, projects, team structures, ownership, and decisions that influence future work. Repeated preferences: habits, writing style, recurring choices, and the way someone likes an update structured. Commitments and deadlines, kept active for as long as they remain relevant. And information requested again and again, which stays longer because ongoing use is evidence that it continues to have value.

## 5. What it ought not to retain

Kivi must not build permanent understanding from isolated searches, minor events, temporary preferences or unverified statements. Expressions of uncertainty — "maybe", "I think", "not sure" — do not constitute facts. One inferred behaviour is not a pattern. Completed tasks first go inactive, then reduce to a minimal record, so an expired deadline never subtly influences a future answer. Silence is not consent: a blank must never be taken to mean yes.

## 6. How it decides

What the user states explicitly takes priority over anything Kivi infers. Inferences must rest on repeated observations before they are accepted. Retention is determined by relevance, recency, reuse, stated importance and confidence, not by storage for its own sake. When new information contradicts something Kivi already holds, it does not overwrite. It raises a clarification, keeps both versions visible, and lets the user choose; the rejected version is marked superseded rather than deleted.

## 7. Trust, consent, and control

The user gives consent once, at the beginning — Kivi does not ask on every interaction, and it does not record things without them knowing. Turn memory off and dictation still works, but nothing new is stored permanently. The user also decides how much authority Kivi has: suggest only, ask before acting, or act automatically within granted limits. Where evidence is weak, Kivi abstains rather than guessing. Staying in control should never require becoming a system administrator.

## 8. Transparency

Every answer and action carries a brief source indicator, for example "Atlas · Slack · Decision". Expanded, it shows the memory used, the original interaction, the app, the project, the date, the exact excerpt, and whether Kivi created, updated or conflicted with that memory. This is the true point of distinction. The promise is not that Kivi remembers; it is that Kivi remembers only what it can support, and can always show you the reasons why.
